Jet A‑1 powers global aviation. Storing it cleanly protects everyone.

Jet A‑1 is the kerosene‑grade fuel used by most turbine‑engine aircraft worldwide. Burning it produces emissions, and poorly managed storage adds more risk on top: water and microbial contamination, leaks, vapour losses and wasted fuel.

Elroi Aviation Consult is led by a dedicated clean aviation fuel consultant. The goal is simple: optimise the clean storage of Jet A‑1 so every operator, whatever its size or location, can deliver safe, on‑specification fuel while reducing its environmental footprint.

Clean fuel, from tank to wing

Fuel quality is won or lost at every step between the storage tank and the aircraft. Choose a stage to see what we review there.

Receipt and storage

Most contamination enters or settles here. We look at how fuel is received, how long it settles, and how water is kept out of the tank.

  • Receipt checks and documentation
  • Settling time before release
  • Routine water draining from tank sumps
  • Tank condition, coatings and venting
  • Bunding, secondary containment and leak detection

Filtration

Filter water separators remove free water and particulates before fuel moves on. They only work if they are sized, monitored and changed correctly.

  • Filter sizing against flow rate
  • Differential pressure (ΔP) monitoring and trending
  • Element change criteria and records
  • Vessel draining routine

Sampling and testing

Sampling is how you prove fuel is clean. We check that sample points, frequency and record‑keeping would stand up to an audit.

  • Sample point locations and access
  • Visual and water checks at the right intervals
  • Microbial growth monitoring
  • Retained samples and traceable records

Metering and transfer

Moving fuel from the farm to refuellers or hydrants is another point where water, dirt and losses can creep in.

  • Meter calibration and reconciliation
  • Hose, coupling and pipework condition
  • Loss and variance tracking
  • Product segregation, including SAF blends

Into‑plane fuelling

Refueller operations are the final link. We review loading, bonding, filtration, sampling and record‑keeping so clean fuel reaches the wing every time.

  • Refueller loading and daily checks
  • Bonding and static control
  • On‑vehicle filtration and final samples
  • Delivery records and handover

Clean aviation shouldn’t be a privilege.

Airports and operators in economically stretched regions often face the greatest fuel‑quality and environmental risks with the fewest resources, yet aviation there carries emergency response, medical flights, trade and connection.

We put particular focus on these communities. We design low‑cost, phased and locally maintainable solutions, starting with the improvements that deliver the biggest safety, quality and emissions gains.

Cleaner storage means less wasted fuel and fewer costly contamination events, which also makes a stronger case for further investment.

Questions operators ask

We are a small airfield. Is this for us?

Yes. Recommendations are scaled to your throughput and budget. A single‑tank site gets a short, practical action plan, not an international hub’s programme.

What does a fuel storage audit involve?

An on‑site review of your tanks, pipework, filtration, sampling and records, followed by a written report that ranks each finding by risk and cost to fix.

Do you replace our existing fuel supplier or quality team?

No. We advise and train. Your team and suppliers keep ownership of operations; we help them run to a higher, documented standard.

Can you help us prepare for sustainable aviation fuel?

Yes. We review segregation, compatibility and handling so your facility can take SAF blends without putting fuel quality at risk.

Do you work outside our country?

Yes. We serve clients worldwide, combining on‑site visits with remote follow‑up.
